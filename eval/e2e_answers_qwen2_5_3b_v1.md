## 4. [answerable/interactions] Can granisetron be used safely alongside other medicines?
- abstained: False (answered), uncited: False
- cited sections: ['drug_interactions']

Granisetron does not induce or inhibit the cytochrome P-450 drug-metabolizing enzyme system in vitro, and it has been safely administered with various drugs. However, because granisetron is metabolized by hepatic cytochrome P-450 drug-metabolizing enzymes, inducers or inhibitors of these enzymes may change the clearance and, hence, the half-life of granisetron.
