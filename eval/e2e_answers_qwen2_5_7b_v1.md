## 11. [answerable/side_effects] What unwanted effects might irbesartan cause?
- abstained: False (answered), uncited: False
- cited sections: ['warnings', 'overdosage', 'drug_interactions']

Irbesartan can cause unwanted effects such as hypotension, which requires correction of volume or salt depletion before administration [1]. It may also lead to renal function impairment and elevated serum potassium levels [1,2]. Additionally, irbesartan can cause fetal harm, including fetal and neonatal morbidity and death, and potential adverse effects in infants [1,2]. Notably, overdosage is unlikely to cause severe manifestations other than hypotension and tachycardia [3]. Drug interactions, particularly with lithium and nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), can increase the risk of renal impairment and hyperkalemia [4].

## 12. [answerable/side_effects] What unwanted effects might alprazolam cause?
- abstained: False (answered), uncited: False
- cited sections: ['adverse_reactions', 'use_in_specific_populations', 'boxed_warning']

Alprazolam can cause adverse behavioral effects such as stimulation, increased muscle spasticity, sleep disturbances, hallucinations, agitation, rage, irritability, and aggressive or hostile behavior [1]. These effects are reported rarely, and patients with certain conditions like borderline personality disorder, a history of violent or aggressive behavior, or alcohol/substance abuse may be at risk [1]. Additionally, alprazolam can cause sedation, poor feeding, and poor weight gain in infants exposed through breast milk [3]. The drug also carries risks of abuse, misuse, and addiction, which can lead to overdose or death, often in combination with other substances [4].
